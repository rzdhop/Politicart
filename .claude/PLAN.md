## Context

Why: the owner finds the five circuits interchangeable ("toutes les maps se ressemblent"), the races static (same obstacles every time, little feedback on items and powers), the Politibox too plain, team races laggy, and loads slow. The 3D character select (PR #13/#14) set the quality bar; the races and the economy screens have to reach it.

What the exploration found (details in the reports; the facts the plan relies on):

- **One construction for all circuits** (`apps/game/src/engine/track.js` `buildTrack`): a flat Catmull-Rom loop of 13–14 points in a ~350 m box at `scale: 3`, one 30 m road (`CFG.roadHalfWidth = 15`, `state.js`), the same kerb/pavement/barrier strips, the same shared furniture (a lamp every 36 samples, 8 ad boards, one grandstand, 4 crowd zones, 4-urn rows), one feature vocabulary (`pad`, `ramp`, `pit`, `crossing`, `chicane`). Only the road texture (asphalt/cobble/gravel), four colours and the themed decor builder differ. Lighting and fog distances are identical on every track (`race.js:79-83`); `track.ground` is a dead field. No elevation (Montmartre's climb is ramps in series, A-035), no banking, no width change, no time of day, no weather.
- **Nothing varies between two races on the same map.** `features` are static; chicane blocks are pushed into `G.hazards` at build with no `id`; every random draw goes through `util.js rand()/pick()` = `Math.random`, unseeded. No seed exists in solo (`race.js startRace`), on the server (`race-worker.js workerData`, `server/race.js` `{t:'race'}`) or on clients (`netrace.js beginNetRace`).
- **Latent client/server desync:** `cyclists.js buildCyclists` spawns a random *number* of riders; snapshots match cyclists by index. The server's `fauchage` removes chicane obstacles from its `G.hazards` while clients keep theirs.
- **Textures:** 7 canvas textures at 256 px (`textures.js`, 45 lines), no normal maps on decor. `fabric.js` already has fBm noise (`fbm`) and seeded albedo+normal generation (`fabricPixels`) with size tiers. `primitives.textPlane` allocates a 512 px canvas + material per call (dozens per race, never disposed); `disposeScene` frees geometries only.
- **Multiplayer:** 60 Hz worker, 20 Hz JSON snapshots of everything (~33 fields/kart, hazards relisted every tick, no quantisation except cyclists), client renders 6 ticks (100 ms) behind, render clock re-anchored on every snapshot arrival (`netrace.js:150`), `bracket()` clamps (karts freeze then jump on a late packet), **no local prediction** (CHECKPOINT "known limits"), inputs at 30 Hz without sequence numbers, input handled behind the per-connection async queue (`server.js:371`), the snapshot stringified once per player (`server/race.js:36-40`), no `bufferedAmount` check, no RTT measurement. No channel for one-shot visual effects (the Mégaphone ring is built inside the server worker and never seen by clients; Amendement's swap is an un-flagged teleport).
- **Loading:** one monolithic bundle (no dynamic import, no `manualChunks`), no splash, the server sends no `Cache-Control`/ETag/compression; the race scene (track, decor, 6 karts+pilots, shaders) is built synchronously in `startField`; nothing is pre-built while the player sits on the circuits screen or in the lobby; the server builds the full decor headless on every race. DEC-020 holds the clock up to 10 s for slow clients (4–8 s measured).
- **Items/powers visuals:** about two thirds have no visual of their own (status `slowed/confused/capped/rabot/slip/relance` are drawn nowhere and are absent from snapshots); the HUD has no keyframes; `payload.js` carries no hit/boost/status flags. Reusable: `trail.js` (pooled particle system, headless-safe), `lootbox.js burst()/fly()`, `hemicycle.js` confetti, `race.js:144` bump camera shake.
- **Politibox:** a clean timed phase machine (`lootbox.js` PHASES/PHASE_TIME, `onPhase`, skip by tap) on the legacy race look; a bug at `lootbox.js:318` (`lidSpin` is inside a comment: the lid never tumbles); no sound, no reduced-motion, no governor. Reusable: `hemicycle.js buildStageScene` (+ `burst()`), `stagelook.js withLook`, `preview.js buildRig/disposeRig`, the frame governor, `warmStage`.
- **Design system:** `packages/ui` with one story + `index.ts` export + docs description + group test per component (`stories/coverage.test.ts`); motion only in `fx.css`, off under reduced motion; element ids are a contract (DEC-009). Off-system screens: `LobbyScreen` (misuses `AdminGrid`, `ResultsTable` as player list, buttons instead of `ChoiceGroup`), `ShopScreen` (raw odds table/trail list in `game.css`), `ResultsScreen` (no podium), `TrackCard` preview is a 2D spline outline with no meta.
- **Budgets on record:** race frame ≤ "today + 10 %" (`PLAN.md` §4.2: ≈1,550 calls / 450k tris on high, ≈365 / 66k on "légers"); measured Élysée high 1,259 / 319k, low 254–271 calls / 84k tris (DEC-047); `headless.test.js` bounds a circuit to 1,500..9,000 meshes and >8 cyclists; the "légers" fold test builds twice under a mocked `Math.random` and expects identical triangles; tracks are referenced by index (append only); `data.test.js` pins the track ids and the feature type list.

## Wave 1 (parallel worktrees, file-disjoint)

### Stage 1 — Circuit foundation: seed, layouts, elevation, width, cyclists (engine + server plumbing)

Files: `engine/seed.js` (new), `util.js`, `data.js`, `state.js`, `track.js` (road/features/cyclists only), `cyclists.js`, `physics.js` (ground height), `ai.js`, `minimap.js`, `race.js` (`startRace`, `startField` signature), `netrace.js` (`beginNetRace`), `netstate.js` (hazard ids), `server/src/race.js`, `race-worker.js`, `lobby.js`; tests `data.test.js`, `headless.test.js`, `netstate.test.js`, `netrace.test.js`, `server/race.test.js`, `race-cast.test.js`.

1. **Seeded PRNG** `engine/seed.js`: `makeRng(seed)` (mulberry32), `rngRand/rngPick` helpers; `G.seed`, `G.rng`. Gameplay-relevant draws (layout choice, obstacle placement, cyclist count/spawn/base speed, crossing phase, AI lane/skill, item-box variants) use `G.rng`. Cosmetic draws keep `Math.random`. The fold test's `Math.random` mock stays valid because both builds also pass the same seed.
2. **Seed plumbing:** solo `startRace()` draws `seed = (Math.random()*2**32)>>>0`; server `runRace` draws it, puts it in `workerData` and in every `{t:'race', trackIdx, seed, roster, you}` (both sends, `addGod` included); `beginNetRace` reads `m.seed`; `startField(slots, {seed})`. Tests that build races pass a fixed seed.
3. **Layout pool in `data.js`:** each track gains `layouts: [{ id, name, features: [...], cyclists: n, climate: 'dawn'|'noon'|'dusk'|'night' }]` (3 named variants per circuit, e.g. Élysée « Visite officielle », « Jour de manif », « Travaux du Faubourg »), the base `features` staying the invariant skeleton (start, urn rows, the big ramps). `chooseLayout(track, rng)` merges base + variant; the variant name reaches the HUD at race start (`CenterAnnounce` under the countdown) and the lobby (`room.view()` carries nothing: the seed is drawn at start, so the name shows at the countdown only).
4. **New feature types** (`data.test.js` list updated): `obstacle` (static road obstacles with `kind`: `sedan`, `van`, `barrier`, `stall`, `bins`, `horse`, `scaffold`, `fountain`), `narrow` (pinch: per-sample half-width from `w` over `len`), `bank` (camber angle over `len`), `wind` (lateral push zone, Marseille mistral), `bump` (a short hump of height `h`), `tunnel` (visual + darker fog band), `hill` handled by the elevation profile below. Physics: `narrow` → `G.samples[i].hw`; `bank` → `G.samples[i].bank` applied to `m.rotation.z`; `wind` → lateral velocity in `updateKart` when not airborne; `bump` → `groundY` like ramps without launch unless fast.
5. **Elevation profile:** each track gets `profile: [[f, y], ...]` (control points, smoothed by Catmull-Rom over the lap, closed) → `G.samples[i].y`, `G.samples[i].slope`. `updateKart` reads `groundY = s.y + rampY`; `k.y` becomes absolute; leaving a crest at speed launches like a ramp end (`slope` drop > threshold). The road ribbon, strips, bike lane, start line, item boxes, features and cyclists (`placeCyclist`) take `s.y`. Decor builders get `yAt(x, z)` (nearest-sample y) from `track.js`. Minimap shades by height. Camera `ty` adds the kart's `y`. Montmartre becomes a real climb (+22 m); Bourbon's bridges become real humps; Marseille climbs to the Corniche; Versailles has two terraces; Élysée stays flat but banked at the cour d'honneur.
6. **Per-track width:** `track.roadHalfWidth` (Élysée 13, Bourbon 15, Versailles 17, Montmartre 11, Marseille 14) → `CFG.roadHalfWidth` becomes `G.hw` set in `buildTrack`; every reader (`physics.js`, `cyclists.js`, `track.js`, `ai.js`, `items.js`, `powers.js`, `minimap.js`) reads `G.hw` or `s.hw`. Bike lane offsets become relative to `hw`.
7. **Static obstacles with ids:** chicane blocks and `obstacle` features get `id: nextEntityId()` at build (deterministic: the `G.entitySeq` reset moves before `buildTrack`, the counter moves to `state.js`). The client's hazard map starts from the built scene's static ids and drops an obstacle the snapshot no longer lists (fauchage). The snapshot keeps its full hazard list (Annex A, point 7).
8. **Cyclists:** count and spawns from `layout.cyclists` and `G.rng` (deterministic); more riders (12–28 per layout instead of ~15); new whims: `peloton` (3 riders in file that swerve together), `delivery` (fast, cuts across), `cargo` (slow, wide), `wrongWay` kept. Visual variety (bike kinds) is stage 7's.
9. Tests: `seed.test.js` (determinism), `data.test.js` (layouts, profile monotone-closed, widths, feature types), `headless.test.js` (every track × every layout builds and races 30 s; deterministic cyclist count; same seed → identical `G.hazards` ids/positions on two builds; mesh ceiling 14,000), `netstate.test.js` (hazard events), `server/race.test.js` (seed in `{t:'race'}` and in `workerData`, same seed on `addGod`).

### Stage 3 — Netcode: prediction, reconciliation, adaptive interpolation, lighter protocol

Files: `netrace.js`, `netstate.js`, `net.js`, `physics.js` (`updateKart(k, dt, {replay})` guards), `race.js` (`simStep` unchanged), `server/src/race-worker.js`, `server/src/race.js`, `server/src/server.js`, `payload.js` (`ping`), HUD `LatencyReadout` (packages/ui hud) + `Hud.tsx`/`game.js`; tests `netrace.test.js`, `netstate.test.js`, `server/race.test.js`, `server.test.js`.

(Architecture detail from the design agent is appended in **Annex B** below.)

1. **Inputs with sequence numbers** at 60 Hz (every client step, coalesced on the wire to ≤ 60/s): `{t:'input', seq, c, item, power}`; the worker applies the latest and echoes `ack[slot] = seq` in the snapshot header (`ak: {slot: seq}`), plus the server tick the input was applied at.
2. **Local prediction:** the client keeps an input history; each frame it re-simulates its own kart from the last acked server state with fixed 1/60 steps through `updateKart(k, STEP, {replay: true})`, where `replay` suppresses `say/hintFor/sfxFor`, item rolls, lap/finish side effects, and the AI/collision/pickup passes (only `updateKart` + the wall/ramp/pit/pad geometry runs). Discrete state (item, stun, boost, lap, rank, finish) stays server-authoritative and is copied from the snapshot. Error smoothing: position/heading error blended out over 120 ms, snapped above 3 m or a stun/teleport flag.
3. **Teleport flag** `tp` on the kart (Amendement, Pantouflage, god commands) so `lerpKart` and the smoother snap instead of dragging.
4. **Adaptive interpolation for other karts:** a smoothed server-clock offset (EMA of `tk` vs arrival), delay = max(2 intervals, p95 jitter + 1 interval), capped at 250 ms; `bracket()` extrapolates up to 100 ms past the last snapshot with velocity (`s`, `h`) before clamping.
5. **RTT:** `{t:'ping', id}` / `{t:'pong', id}` every 2 s, RTT/jitter in `payload.ping`, a small `LatencyReadout` in the HUD top-right (`#hud-ping`, hidden in solo).
6. **Server path:** `input` handled synchronously outside `c.queue`; snapshot stringified once with per-player `ev/fx` spliced (`JSON.stringify` once, string concat per player); skip a snapshot for a socket whose `bufferedAmount` > 64 KB; `SNAP_EVERY = 2` (30 Hz) as a measured option, kept only if CPU allows 4 concurrent races on the Render free instance.
7. **Protocol diet:** positions to mm, angles to 1e-4, speeds to 1e-2 (`netstate` quantisers, tests with tolerances); HUD-only fields (`it ir pc bl ft lap pr`) only to their owner via the per-slot `me` block; crossings derived from `G.time` (no `c` field); cyclists unchanged; hazards stay a full list (Annex A).
8. Tests: determinism (`netrace.test.js` harness: replaying the recorded inputs on the client reproduces the server kart within 1e-6 per step), reconciliation (an injected correction converges within 120 ms, snaps on `tp`), adaptive delay (jitter profiles), protocol round-trips, `server.test.js` ping/pong and input bypass of the queue.

## Annex A — Seeded layouts: design detail (stage 1)

Verified facts that shape it: `G.entitySeq = 0` is reset in `startField` **after** `buildTrack` (`race.js:91` vs `:85`), so ids given at build would collide with later `spawnHazard` ids → move the reset before `buildTrack`. `nextEntityId` lives in `items.js:33` and `track.js` must not import `items.js` (cycle) → move the counter to `state.js`. `buildElysee` reads `G.track.features` (`track.js:550`) → read `G.layout.features`. `mergeByMaterial` keeps the root group, so a `still()`-marked obstacle group stays removable on "légers". The fold test builds twice under a mocked `Math.random`: seeded draws must never go through `Math.random` and `buildTrack` needs a deterministic default seed (0).

1. **`engine/seed.js`**: `mulberry32(seed)`, `makeRng(seed)` → `{ next, rand, pick, int, shuffle }`, `derive(seed, salt)` (sub-streams so adding a draw to one builder never moves another's), `newSeed()` (uint32, JSON/structuredClone safe), salts `SALT_LAYOUT/SALT_FEATURES/SALT_CYCLISTS`.
2. **`state.js`**: `G.seed`, `G.layout`, `G.entitySeq`, `export const nextEntityId`.
3. **`data.js`**: `features` stays the fixed skeleton (big ramps and their pits, the crossing); new `variants: [{ group, pick, of: [feature | { set: [features] }] }]` (groups: `chicane`, `street` obstacles, `pads`, `crossing` speed/range, `jump`, `climate`) and `cyclists: { min, max }` (min > 8 keeps `headless.test.js:96`). The classic chicane is the first member of its group, so today's layout stays one of the variants. At most one `crossing` group per track (crossings are snapshot by index: the count must be fixed per layout). Layout variants get a `name` (shown at the countdown) and the elevation `profile` + `roadHalfWidth` of stage 1 are per track, not per variant.
4. **`engine/layout.js`** (pure): `chooseLayout(track, seed)` → `{ seed, name, features, picks, climate, cyclists }`; picks sorted by index so build order (and ids) never depends on draw order. `data.test.js` enforces the schema (`pick <= of.length`, `|lat| <= hw - 3` for obstacles, `set` members carry `f`).
5. **`track.js`**: `buildTrack(scene, track, { fold, seed = 0 })` sets `G.seed/G.layout`; `buildFeatures(scene, layout, rng)` with `rng = makeRng(derive(seed, SALT_FEATURES))` (crossing phase `t`, chicane `side`, obstacle kinds: `sedan`, `stall`, `barrier` via a factored `vaubanPanel`); every chicane block and `obstacle` gets `{ id: nextEntityId(), type: 'obstacle', kind, ... }`. `buildCyclists(scene, rng, n)` with an exact count `n`, evenly spread with seeded jitter; bike cosmetics stay on `Math.random`. All 73 other `rand/pick` calls in the decor stay cosmetic.
6. **Seed on the wire**: `runRace(room, roster, io, { fast, hold, seed = newSeed() })` → `{ t:'race', trackIdx, roster, you, seed }` (both sends, `addGod` included) and `workerData.seed`; `race-worker.js` and `beginNetRace` pass `{ seed }` to `startField`; solo `startRace` draws one. `PROTOCOL` → 2; a `race` message without `seed` aborts with « SERVEUR TROP ANCIEN ».
7. **Client hazards**: `beginNetRace` seeds `netrace.hazards` from `G.hazards` (static ids) and keeps `staticIds`; after each sync `G.hazards` is filtered to the ids the snapshot still lists (fauchage removal now reaches clients); `clearEntities` spares static ids (the results screen keeps the circuit). The snapshot keeps the full hazard list (≈30 bytes per static obstacle; absence is the removal signal), so stage 1 does **not** introduce hazard add/remove events.
8. Tests: `seed.test.js`, `layout.test.js` (every track × seeds 0..199: exact `pick` counts, skeleton prefix, determinism, two seeds differ, cyclists in range, exactly one crossing), `headless.test.js` (same seed twice → identical `G.layout`, hazard ids/positions, cyclist count, crossing phases, ramps/pits/pads; the fold test passes an explicit seed), `netstate.test.js` (every hazard has a unique integer id), `netrace.test.js` (seeded `startField`/`beginNetRace`; a mown static obstacle leaves the scene and `G.hazards`), `server/race.test.js` and `race-cast.test.js` (seed in the start message, in `workerData`, same seed on the god's re-send), `gameplay.test.js` helper passes a seed whose obstacles avoid the samples the tests place karts at.

## Annex B — Prediction and reconciliation: design detail (stage 3)

Verified facts: the worker applies latest-wins controls per tick and the client sends at 30 Hz or on change, so a bare `ack = seq` would over-replay by 1–3 ticks → the worker also reports `n`, how many ticks it has applied the acked controls. `updateKart` is self-contained per kart except `updateProgress` (laps, finish, `say`, `sfxFor`, `G.resultsAt`), `say/hintFor/sfxFor`, `stunKart`, the god item roll and `updateKartVisual`. The replay needs fields the snapshot lacks (`pitGrace`, `padGrace`, `hazardGrace`, `kx`, `kz`, `slowed`, `confused`, `slip`, `rabot`, `capped`, `relance`, `relanceTimer`, `boostPower`, `maxLap`) → an owner-only block `me`. The client must go to `race` from the newest snapshot, not the delayed bracket, or prediction zeroes the throttle for 100 ms then snaps.

1. **Protocol**: `{ t:'input', seq, c, item, power }`; `{ t:'ping', n, at }` / `{ t:'pong', n, at }`; the owner's snapshot message gains `ack`, `n`, `me` (`encodeKartPrivate`); public kart floats quantised (`x z y` to mm, `h st` to 1e-4, `s vy dc pc pr bu` to 1e-2); HUD-only fields (`it ir pc bl ft lap pr`) move to `me` after a grep confirms no other-kart reader; a `tp` teleport flag (Amendement, Pantouflage, god moves) makes `lerpKart` and the smoother snap.
2. **Engine guards**: `G.replaying`; `physics.js` `updateProgress` returns early (idx/progress only), the god roll and `updateKartVisual` are skipped; `events.js` (`emit/say/tell/hintFor`) and `audio.js` (`sfx/sfxFor`) return at once while replaying; `STEP = 1/60` exported and shared with the worker; `netstate.js` splits `applyKart` into `applyKartMotion` + `applyKartDiscrete` and adds `encodeKartPrivate/applyKartPrivate`.
3. **`engine/predict.js`**: input history (≤ 240), `simulateLocal(k, c)` (swaps `k.ctrl`, sets the flag, restores both), `predictTick`, `reconcile(k, s, me, ack, n)` (apply server motion + private block, drop history ≤ ack and the first `n-1` entries, replay the rest, then `applyKartDiscrete`; the on-screen-vs-new difference becomes an error offset, snapped above 3 m / 0.6 rad or on the first reconcile), `blend(dt)` (≈95 % gone in 100 ms), `estimateDelay(devs)` (2 snapshot intervals + 2.5 σ of arrival jitter, clamped to 4..18 ticks, moved by at most one tick per second so the render clock never jumps).
4. **`netrace.js`**: fixed-step prediction of the local kart from `readInput()` (≤ 4 steps per frame), the other karts interpolated behind the adaptive delay with short velocity extrapolation in `bracket`, the error offset applied only around `updateKartVisual`/`viewStep` and removed afterwards (the camera and the minimap see the blended pose; the prediction state is never contaminated), `payload.net = { rtt, delay, lead }`, `transition('race')` from the newest snapshot in `receiveSnap`, a ping every second.
5. **Server**: `race-worker.js` keeps `seq/applied/n` per human (reordered inputs dropped) and posts `per[slot] = { ev, fx, ack, n, me }`; `server/race.js` stringifies the snapshot once and splices per-player parts as strings (`io.sendRaw`); `server.js` handles `input` and `ping` synchronously before the per-connection queue, `sendRaw` skips a socket whose `bufferedAmount` > 64 KB; `SNAP_EVERY = 2` is measured, not assumed.
6. **HUD**: `#h-net` readout (RTT · delay) in the top-left corner, empty in solo; later a dedicated `LatencyReadout` component in stage 8.
7. Tests: `predict.test.js` (replay determinism within 1e-6 in-process and 2e-3 m through JSON quantisation; the water-pit negative control proving the private block matters; silence during replay; `reconcile` bookkeeping for `ack/n`; smoothing and snapping; `estimateDelay`), `netrace.test.js` harness extended with a scripted local kart and `per` blocks (predicted kart ahead of the delayed field and within 2 m of the re-simulated newest snapshot; other karts unchanged; seq non-decreasing; green from the newest snapshot; delay moves ≤ 1 tick per second), `netstate.test.js` round-trips, `server/race.test.js` (`ack/n/me` to the right player; `sendRaw` path), `server.test.js` (pong; an input sent right after `hello` still reaches the race), E2E multiplayer `#h-net` shows milliseconds.

Pitfalls: stage 1 is a prerequisite (the replay reads the server's ramps/pits/pads); `applyKartDiscrete` must run after the replay or `itemRolling` reaching 0 sets a null item; the last-ulp drift between browser and Node is hidden by the blend; a client below 60 fps replays fewer ticks and shows less lead, never a jump; early inputs before `join` resolves are dropped (DEC-020 tolerates it).
